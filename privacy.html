Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 30, 2026

WTMP Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application handles information, specifically regarding the sensitive permissions required for security monitoring.

1. Camera Usage (Critical Policy)

Permission: android.permission.CAMERA

Purpose: To capture photos of individuals who attempt to unlock your device or successfully unlock it while protection is active.

Data Handling: All photos are stored locally on your device's internal storage. We do not transmit, upload, or share these photos with any servers or third parties. We have no access to your captured media.

2. Device Administrator & Security

This app uses the Device Administrator permission (BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN). This is required to detect failed lock screen attempts (wrong PIN/Pattern/Password). No other administrative actions are taken.

3. Application Activity Data

The app may collect a list of applications launched during a monitoring session to provide you with a detailed security report. This data is stored only on your device and is never shared.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

All data (photos, timestamps, app lists) is stored in a local database on your phone. You have absolute control over this data and can delete it at any time via the "Purge Logs" feature in the app settings.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
