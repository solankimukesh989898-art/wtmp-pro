Data Deletion & Safety Guide

At WTMP Pro, we ensure that you have complete control over your security data. Since we do not store your data on our servers, deletion is handled directly within the app.

How to Permanently Delete Your Data

  1. Launch the WTMP Pro app on your device.
  2. Tap on the Settings (gear icon) in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the System Management section.
  4. Tap the Purge All Logs button.
  5. Confirm the action in the popup dialog.

Warning: This action will irreversibly delete all security events, reports, and captured photos from your device storage.

Uninstallation

Uninstalling the app will typically remove the local database, but we recommend using the "Purge All Logs" feature first to ensure all captured media files are cleared from the device's storage directories.
